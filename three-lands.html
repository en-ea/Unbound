The Three Lands

Three peoples, three lands. You live on the mainland, you reach a second land, and together you restore the third, which was taken along with its people. You end up as the one who ties them together, with companions you choose. Pick what feels right in each section and add notes. Saves automatically

Quick Zelda refresher, so you know what's similar

Breath of the Wild: one land (Hyrule). An evil called Calamity Ganon wrecked it 100 years ago. You wake from a long sleep and free four giant machines across the regions. The peoples (Zora, Gorons, Rito, Gerudo) aren't fighting each other. They're just recovering.
Tears of the Kingdom: the same Hyrule plus sky islands above and huge Depths below, so three layers of one land. An ancient people (the Zonai) vanished long ago and left their tech behind. A dark "gloom" spreads from below.
Older ones: Wind Waker: Hyrule was flooded and sank under the sea, leaving only islands. Skyward Sword: people were lifted to live above the clouds. Twilight Princess: a banished people live in a shadow realm, and their twilight covers parts of the land.
What's NOT Zelda about ours: three separate peoples who are split and at odds, a whole people who were taken and come back, you becoming the hero through what you build and master rather than being chosen, and an endless gather-and-craft life loop instead of a story that ends.
