## Year 37: The Burning of Lettice

That season people saw a stillborn foal, and began to watch one another. Whispers started that Lettice had cursed the village - charms of twisted straw found by a doorway. Wat named Lettice before the elder. Godric, the elder, heard it in the square. They put Lettice in the millpond. They floated. Guilty. The sentence was the fire. They burned Lettice at the stake at dusk; the smoke was seen from the next valley. A few years later, Wat stood up in the shrine and took it all back: there had been no curse, only fear, and they had named Lettice for it. The truth came too late for Lettice. Now there are candles on Lettice's grave, and strangers come to kneel. They are remembered as Lettice the Blessed.
