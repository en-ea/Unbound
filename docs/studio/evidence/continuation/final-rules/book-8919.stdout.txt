# The Tales of Harrowmere, years 1-1000

*Told from the village's own memory: every name, cause and turn below happened in the simulation (seed 8919); only the sentence patterns were written by hand.*

## Year 8: Bettony Walks Out

That season people saw crows on the church roof, and began to watch one another. Whispers started that Bettony had cursed the village - charms of twisted straw found by a doorway. Agnes named Bettony before the elder. Orla, the elder, heard it in the square. They put Bettony in the millpond. They floated. Guilty. The law asked for the fire, but the elder, weary of blood, gave exile. Bettony walked out of the south gate with one bundle and did not look back. They are remembered as Bettony Who Walked Out.

## Year 45: Tam in the Pillory

Rafe had never forgiven Simkin for marrying Elsbet. One evening old grudges came to blows: Rafe struck Simkin. Simkin named Rafe before the elder. Ansel, the elder, heard it in the square. The sentence was exile. Tam had never forgiven Simkin for what was done to Rafe. One evening old grudges came to blows: Tam struck Simkin. Simkin named Tam before the elder. Agnes, the elder, heard it in the square. Tam confessed, and was spared the worst. The sentence was a day in the pillory. Tam stood a day in the pillory while the village muttered. They are remembered as Tam Who Confessed.

## Year 51: The Night at the Stone

That was the year the storm came down on the Hale house. When the air cleared, the family was gone, and in their place stood people in furs who spoke like the old songs - the Hales' own forebears. The forebears read the strange world as their gods' anger. At dusk they took Cecily to the stake, and the drums began. Before dawn Tobin crept to the stake and cut the ropes, and the two of them ran for the houses.

## Year 343: The Offering of Bram

That was the year the storm came down on the Miller house. When the air cleared, the family was gone, and in their place stood people in furs who spoke like the old songs - the Millers' own forebears. The forebears read the strange world as their gods' anger. At dusk they took Bram to the stake, and the drums began. At dawn Brynn made the offering, as their fathers had. They kept the heart for their fire. Piers named Brynn before the elder. Colm, the elder, heard it in the square. Brynn confessed, and was spared the worst. The sentence was the fire. But before the day came, the mist rolled back over the round house, and Brynn was gone with it, back to their own time. They are remembered as Brynn Who Confessed.

## Year 679: The Empty Cell

Then came a two-headed calf. The old women said it meant a curse among them. Whispers started that Sella had cursed the village - charms of twisted straw found by a doorway. Edric named Sella before the elder. Edric, the elder, judged it too. They put Sella in the millpond. They floated. Guilty. The sentence was the fire. But on the night before, Sella's cell was found empty and the ropes cut. Everyone knew it was Tobin, and no one said so. Sella was gone by morning, into the woods. They are remembered as Sella Who Walked Out and Tobin the Brave.

## Year 877: The Late Truth About Nell

That season people saw a two-headed calf, and began to watch one another. Whispers started that Nell had cursed the village - charms of twisted straw found by a doorway. Ansel named Nell before the elder. Hob, the elder, heard it in the square. They put Nell in the millpond. They floated. Guilty. The law asked for the fire, but the elder, weary of blood, gave exile. A few years later, Ansel stood up in the shrine and took it all back: there had been no curse, only fear, and they had named Nell for it. The truth came out. They sent riders after Nell, and Nell came home through the south gate, thinner and older; the village came out to meet them, ashamed. They are remembered as Nell Who Came Home.
