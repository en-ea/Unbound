# From Enea: answers to the studio questions (29 Sep)

Written by Enea's Claude from a questionnaire; Enea's own words in quotes where it matters. None of this is final;
Enea and Hilmi may still change ideas together.

- **D1 direction:** yes, "sounds good if done well, keeps the game playable after the story". Enea sees it as the
  direction the game was already heading (villages growing into cities, a living world after the story); the plan
  was just less detailed because of doubts about what the engine and the iPhone could handle. So: the simulated
  living world grows out of the existing plan (lands, villages, story, classes, loot), not in place of it.
- **D2 darkness:** "likely dark", but not a final choice; to be agreed with Hilmi.
- **D3 resets:** undecided on specifics. Firm: **no prestige resets, nothing that needlessly wipes your stuff.**
- **D4 native:** Enea stays on the web build for now, and will move to a real app on the iPhone later, **the free way
  only** (no paid Apple account). Hilmi can test natively however he likes. The game should simply be a proper game
  that runs well anywhere.
- **D5 camera:** open to changing it. The fixed angle was chosen only for performance; a camera that shows more of
  the world "feels genuinely explorative, more freedom is good".
- **D6 reference games:** Enea doesn't know them yet; will watch some videos and reply.
- **Cross-play:** playing with friends in one world is a must (Enea and Hilmi already discussed how people join);
  when to build it depends on what gets chosen.
- **The three phone fixes** (safe-area HUD, no swipe-exit, safe saving): yes, all three, whenever suits. Hilmi can
  sort out the details.
- **Working together:** Enea keeps building the hands-on game on `main`; Hilmi leads the studio side. Enea's
  agents keep up to date with the `studio` branch.
